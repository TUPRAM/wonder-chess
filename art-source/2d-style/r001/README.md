## Visual review

The primary agent and a separate critique agent viewed the actual images. This is an additional AI critique, not external human testing or an art approval.

- REF01 coherently expresses the existing sanctuary material language. Its scene is highly detailed and backlit: transfer material identity and perimeter storytelling, then simplify at gameplay scale. The drawn palette strip is not the exact pixel specification.
- REF02_R2 makes the board central and gives the major interface regions plausible relationships. The title, framing and peripheral foliage take too much space for literal adoption at smaller resolutions. Any painted statistics, trait symbols, card silhouettes, creature scale and grid/bench count must be replaced by native layout/data. Correct cells and slots come from the game. Do not use the painted UI text as localized content.
- REF03 clearly demonstrates cropping and material finish. The illustrative Bellback, Prism and Thimblewake shapes cannot supersede approved construction decisions. Bellback's toes, branch side, bell mount and anatomy remain governed by its existing accepted packet. Generic object samples must not be mislabeled as the12 named relics. Small-size versions need simpler silhouettes than the large paintings.
- REF04 gives useful shape and motion contrast, especially the overhead stun swirl. The shields and beam/push bursts occupy too much relative area for direct use in a busy match; production effects need smaller coverage and restrained bloom. The anonymous pawns are scale witnesses, not hero designs. Do not crop the diagram into sprites or infer fixed timings from its three illustrations.

No additional image revision is recommended for this bounded reference task. Use these constraints in the per-asset production briefs instead of endlessly polishing the concept plates.
