## Why the local method now passes

1. **The opening is real and independently controlled.** The hidden-globe image retains an open aperture. The upper arc and shallower lower arc are visibly different. The diagnostic iris/pupil remains a gaze aid, while the uniform globe has no embossed circular skin relief.
2. **The flagged corner relation improved.** The screen-right corner in front now turns more continuously rather than finishing as the initial tucked angular cut. A narrow lid rim is readable in front and three-quarter views, including the corner turn. The short crease extending from that corner is reduced in both the ordinary and reversed-light images.
3. **The successful cheek volume was retained.** The upper cheek remains broad and continuous beneath the lower rim. The baseline's repeated horizontal cheek bands and circular orbital crater are absent from the final local study. No broad smoothing trough has replaced them.
4. **The final unsmoothed cage is inspectable.** `face_final_cage_three_quarter.png` exposes the aperture, adjacent rows, finite faces through the corner turns, and outward flow into the surrounding region. There is no visible triangulated fan covering the iris. The smoothed-overlay image still has partially occluded edges, but the separate complete cage view resolves the prior evidence gap.
5. **The improvement survives the diagnostic views.** Front, profile, both supplied oblique views, clay, hidden-globe, and reversed light show the same construction. The local acceptance does not depend only on the most flattering gaze render.

## Remaining defects and mandatory reservations

- The opposite-side view still reveals a small prominence at the flagged corner beside the globe. The initial pointed appearance is softened, but that relation should be rechecked against the nasal/temple planes when the anatomical side and placement are established in head context.
- Three-quarter clay still has a shallow local depression adjacent to that corner. It is reduced and does not cross the upper cheek as the old band did; it is not declared perfectly resolved.
- Lid detail remains simple. This decision validates an editable construction with readable margin thickness and separated cheek volume, not a finished stylized eyelid sculpt.
- This is an intentionally isolated open shell. The absence of an integrated full head is not counted as a local geometry failure. Proportions, anatomical placement, approved identity, nearby nose/jaw regressions, and coherent two-eye expression remain **NOT_REVIEWED**.
- Continuous-head boundary topology, removal of old overlapping skin, welding, symmetry/asymmetry decisions, and resulting temple/nose/jaw seams remain **NOT_REVIEWED**. Joining objects alone would not establish successful integration.
- Mesh internals, exact vertex counts, modifier settings, source hashes, runtime import, deformation, Unreal appearance, and packaging were not verified by this visual reviewer.

## Authorized implication of this decision

The modeling owner may retain this local candidate and use the demonstrated method for internal continuation allowed by MR1. Any subsequent integrated head must receive new front, both three-quarter, profile, and coherent two-eye review. Do not carry this local verdict forward as acceptance of those later states.
