## What visibly works

- The candidate reads as one broad side-swept mass. It starts near the offset frontal part and travels over the forehead toward the side of the head. This broad direction is consistent with the approved crop's leading sweep.
- The surface has a broad convex crest and an independently fitted lower side. The cross-section is an asymmetric flattened wedge/lens, plainly different from a circular tube. The isolated top image also shows that width, depth and direction change along the clump rather than remaining a constant hose section.
- The root closeup is seated against the base instead of floating as an arch. The crest retains volume above that contact. The profile and top views likewise do not show the large unsupported loops visible in the matched baseline.
- The unsmoothed cage views expose a manageable sequence of changing stations and width rows, including a finite broad root end and a controlled narrowing toward the side. No precise vertex count or source technique is inferred from these pictures.
- One dominant volume gives a much clearer hierarchy than the baseline's repeated parallel strands. This is a proof of the single-clump representation, not a claim that removing the rest of the hairstyle improves likeness.

## Specific remaining failure

The narrow **contact directly above the near ear** has a visible sharp triangular blade where the foundation/hairline lip meets the lower edge of the clump.

- In `hair_r001_clay_three_quarter.png`, a thin diagonal triangular shape rises from above the ear into the lower boundary of the sweep.
- In `hair_r001_clay_profile.png`, that contact interrupts the otherwise gradual taper with an angular crossing.
- `hair_r001_cage_three_quarter.png` makes the intersection particularly clear: the pale foundation lip intrudes into the dark-outlined clump edge.

This is not an objection to limited hidden overlap. It is a visible contact defect in the very scalp/temple relationship the proof must demonstrate. The rest of the clump does not need to be re-created to address it.

## One bounded corrective focus

Resolve the final clump stations and the immediately adjoining candidate foundation lip above the ear so they meet in a clean, deliberate overlap or continuous transition. Remove the visible triangular intrusion and retain a finite tapered clump end. Preserve the broad leading sweep, independent crest, root seating and asymmetric section. Do not flatten the entire clump onto the scalp or repair unrelated face geometry.

Verify using the same profile, three-quarter, front and top views plus a closeup centered above the ear. Recapture the cage or isolated silhouette only if the correction changes that geometry. A successful correction will retain the present broad mass while eliminating the sharp crossing in both profile and three-quarter views.
