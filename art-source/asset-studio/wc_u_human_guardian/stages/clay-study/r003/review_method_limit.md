## Eyelid-region result

The spatial transition changes the earlier uniform horizontal cheek band, and removing the embossed iris treatment makes the ocular volumes simpler to inspect. These are useful diagnostic changes, not a likeness pass.

The eyes now sit inside excessively hollow socket-like depressions. The upper/lower lid relationship is still indistinct, and the outer corners terminate in visible horizontal cuts rather than anatomical canthi. Residual radial shading/fluting remains around the lower orbital transitions. The deep outer-eye hollow, especially on the viewer's right in the face image, does not match the reference's controlled brow, lid and cheek planes. The broad socket depression overwhelms the calm expression.

Classification: major facial shape and surface-transition failure. The capture does not support calling the eye region corrected.

## Single supported hair-mass result

The face capture shows one broad raised strip lying closer to the foundation over the upper forehead. Toward the viewer's right temple, it narrows into pinched, crumpled or interrupted-looking transitions rather than continuing as a controlled swept mass. Its section and taper are not consistently maintained through that border region.

The profile is dominated by the smooth foundation and does not demonstrate the desired full swept silhouette. Because this was explicitly a one-mass experiment with other locks hidden, I am not treating the absent complete hairstyle as an accidental missing part. The local test itself nevertheless fails to demonstrate a clean root, continuous supported section, controlled edge and successful termination. The image does not independently establish the precise BVH or geometry cause of the pinching.

Classification: major local hair-construction failure. A support projection is a useful operation only when its result preserves the intended mass; contact alone is not sufficient.

## Recommended boundary and next method

The evidence supports preserving the failed snapshot and returning to the better r002 working state while retaining its existing failed-likeness status. Restoration should be verified by the parent; it was not part of this read-only review. No downstream art stage should inherit approval from either state.

The next useful intervention is a focused artist-authored regional control cage or sculpt pass, with intentional placement of a small number of eyelid, canthus, socket and cheek landmarks. Establish one eye region in front and true profile before extending it. For hair, manually control one solid asymmetrical mass against the skull, including its section, support and end transition, before reusing the method.

Further global formula-grid construction, broader smoothing, increasing density, adding grooves or multiplying similar hair paths is not justified by these results. The method needs direct local shape control and visual judgment. A successful script or projection query does not remove the visible defects, and this report issues no human approval.
