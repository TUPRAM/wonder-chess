## Actual improvements

- **The previous jagged goggle-rim construction is no longer visible.** The large protruding oval rings, stepped bridge edges and conspicuous corner notches from r001/r003 have been replaced by a cleaner almond-shaped eye region. This removes a major distracting surface defect, although the replacement does not yet constitute convincing eyelid anatomy.
- **The lower jaw is broader.** Front and three-quarter views show a firmer lower-face outline with less of the earlier tapered mannequin chin. This is a useful direction toward the adult reference, but the reference's cheek-to-jaw planes are not fully resolved.
- **The frontal hair sweep has more directional subdivision.** It is less dominated by the earlier few very wide flat ribbons. Some lock tips and overlap now establish a clearer direction toward the sides and rear.
- **The shorter braid and tighter shoulder nesting remain intact.** These preserve the useful silhouette improvements from the previous diagnostic revision. They are not new evidence of approved face or hair quality.

## Major remaining face defects

Evidence: `face_final.png`, `front_final.png`, `profile_final.png`, `three_quarter_final.png`.

The eyes still read as convex almond patches fitted onto the face. Their pointed boundaries are clean enough to see, but the reference's upper eyelid thickness, thinner lower lid, inner/outer corner anatomy and recessed ocular relationship are missing. The circular raised iris/pupil treatment resembles a bullseye rather than an eye under lids. This is visible in neutral clay; changing color would not resolve the missing geometry relationship. The current round stare conveys surprise or vacancy more than Ada's calm, alert expression.

Both upper cheeks show conspicuous horizontal shelf-like highlight/crease bands beneath the eyes. The bands persist in front and three-quarter views and interrupt the cheek surface. Their precise cause could involve geometry, normals or another surface treatment; a scene inspection is necessary before attributing a technical cause. They should not be treated as accepted cheekbone planes merely because they produce highlights.

The brows are very thin, almost uniform arcs. They do not reproduce the original's substantial shaped brows or their support from the brow ridge. The nasal bridge, nostril wings, cheek transition, philtrum and mouth corners remain softly blended and generic. The closed mouth is preferable to an open dark cavity, but the reference's deliberate upper/lower lip shape is not captured. The ears still read mainly as narrow hoops, and the jaw-to-neck transition remains mannequin-like in profile.

The broader jaw is a useful proportion correction, not a facial-likeness pass. The primary comparison should remain the original portrait, not just the previous failed model.

## Major remaining hair defects

Evidence: `profile_final.png`, `back_final.png`, `face_final.png`.

The new pieces are rounder and narrower than the previous ribbons, but their regular tubular cross-sections and nearly parallel paths make the hair resemble bundled cables. Profile reveals several high arches detached from the apparent scalp, with obvious gaps under the locks. Back view retains broad smooth cap wedges between clumps and a very regular arrangement feeding into the braid. The temporal filler reads as a broad headband rather than naturally layered hair.

The front tips overlap, but their roots, spacing and cross-sections do not yet establish the reference's asymmetric, interwoven masses. Several abrupt boundaries at the crown remain visible in the face closeup. Adding more grooves or more similar tubes would increase detail without correcting the mass structure.

The compact braid length remains improved. Its repeated flattened lobes and abrupt transition from the rear locks are still mechanical. The original reference has a fuller interwoven rear gathering and a clearer hierarchy from swept mass to braid. That relationship has not yet been constructed convincingly.

## Scope and continuation

This pass makes a real improvement over the r001/r003 eye-region defects and changes the hair construction rather than simply repeating the same groove operation. Those improvements should be preserved as evidence. The likeness and hair-quality requirements nevertheless remain unmet, and this review does not support downstream production on the current head.

Before another broad assembly pass, isolate one eye/socket/cheek and prove its anatomical relationship in neutral front/profile/three-quarter captures. Construct upper/lower lids around a recessed ocular surface, resolve the cheek bands, establish brow mass, and judge expression against the original portrait. This is a more useful next proof than adding iris relief or polishing the entire face.

For hair, establish a small number of larger asymmetric masses that sit against the skull and merge convincingly into the rear gathering; vary and flatten cross-sections where appropriate, eliminate raised cable arches and exposed cap wedges, and only then subdivide the masses into supporting locks. Keep the compact braid length while replacing its mechanical gathering and repeated lobe treatment.

The current deliverable is an inspected, editable revision with documented improvements and unresolved major defects. **It remains a study requiring revision, not an approved Ada.**
