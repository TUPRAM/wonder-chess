## What the new foundation visibly helps

1. The nose has recognisable alar wings and underside, and the philtrum meets actual upper-muzzle volume. The long swept recession and projecting lip shelf that dominated r016 are substantially reduced as a connected anatomical problem.
2. The upper cheek has a more continuous independent volume. The obvious r016 band/bowl relationship between medial socket and cheek is reduced; the new surface looks like connected facial tissue rather than an attached eye patch.
3. The ear has more recognisable thin helix, concha and lobe relationships than the old oval-dish construction.
4. The diagnostic irises in r001-gaze and r002 show upper-lid coverage of the iris. The old widely exposed-globe stare is reduced. This does not establish Ada's final eye shape or gaze expression.

These observations support keeping MPFB as a candidate foundation for further evaluation. They do not establish that its uninspected topology is ready for deformation, that all parametric controls improve the reference fit, or that a generated foundation removes the need for artistic correction.

## r001 versus r002

The changes to lip silhouette and projection are small in front, three-quarter and primary views. The profile suggests a slightly more restrained upper-lip projection, with no conspicuous new regression in the compared images. The mouth still has a continuous dark gap and reads slightly parted, and both lips retain a rounded, pouting relationship. This is only modest change; the requested closed-mouth goal is not achieved.

Retain r002 provisionally because it preserves the foundation's gains without an obvious visual regression, not because it is a materially more accurate likeness than r001. Preserve r001 for comparison. No numerical geometric displacement or actual lip collision/contact claim is made from images alone.
