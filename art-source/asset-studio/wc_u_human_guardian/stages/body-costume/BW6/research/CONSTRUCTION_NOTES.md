# BW6 construction notes — research, not asset acceptance

Reviewed 2026-09-10. Local Blender is 5.1.1; `game/WonderChess.uproject` declares Unreal 5.7. Ada is preserved alpha_24 work, separate from the new wonder_vnext art pilot. No external meshes or images were downloaded and no private artwork was uploaded. Museum descriptions are construction references, not dimensions or a replacement design for Ada.

The practical torso method is a coherent padded envelope with ordered cross-sections, then front/back plate facets and side returns fitted to that envelope. A front-to-back axial ray is only a measurement: near the lateral contour it can miss or encounter the wrong anatomical branch. It cannot define a whole garment by independently moving each vertex. The collar needs a new clean local upper-coat interface with deliberate circumferential correspondence, rather than another extrusion of the old serrated boundary. The front hem dips and the back hem rises at center; the navy waist is a fitted fabric layer, not empty space or a strip hiding a gap.

Candidate body reshaping is authorized in the current assignment, but should address a demonstrated body/padding conflict. Initial BW6 defects are already explained by garment/plate rest surfaces; shrinking the master body would not be a supported diagnosis.
