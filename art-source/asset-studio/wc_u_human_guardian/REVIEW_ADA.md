## What the reference decision authorizes

Approval of this identity and construction interpretation lets the next step start: a completely new editable head, neck, collar, chest, hair and one shoulder in neutral clay. It will be judged in true front, profile and three-quarter renders before completing the body and equipment. No old Ada mesh, rig, material, texture or animation will be loaded as a starting asset.

The bust is an early method checkpoint. It cannot approve the complete hero's forms. Full-body topology, UV/baking, material, rig/skin, all seven clips, LOD, actual Unreal integration, source reimport, packaged encounter verification and final owner release remain dependent stages.
