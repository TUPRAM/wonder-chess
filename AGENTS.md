# Wonder Chess project instructions

## Owner roster rework — 2026-10-03 (supersedes the creature roster below)

- The owner dropped the creature roster. Successor heroes are organised by fantasy **race** and **class** with simple Auto Chess-style stat synergies (2/4 thresholds); the race and class lists are not capped. Character art should be simple and readable: clear large parts, little detail and texture.
- `data/vnext/catalog.json` holds the owner-approved 40-hero / 10-race / 10-class design. Fourteen heroes are executable: seven carry the earlier executed mechanics with unchanged numbers (Shieldbearer, Boar Rusher, Grove Druid, Hookjaw, Prism Scholar, Tide Caller, Soul Jailer) and seven use the standard ability system with untuned design-target stats (Chapel Healer, Hammerer, Earthbreaker, Bearguard, Bomber, Ember Wisp, Blood Drummer). On 2026-10-04 the owner had eleven more made playable through the standard ability system (Siege Master, Longbow Scout, Moonblade, Treant Caller, Ironwall, Rifleman, Forge King, Jinxer, Dartblower, Bonechanter, Foxclaw), so 25 are executable; where the planned ability needs something the engine lacks (summons, blinks, lines, damage over time, drain) the nearest existing effect stands in and the tooltip states what really happens. The other 15 were `authored_not_implemented` with `planned_stats` only until the adoption programme below made all 40 executable on 2026-10-10.
- Relics (owner direction 2026-10-03): all 12 fit every hero and give plain stat benefits with no drawback (skill power, attack damage, attack speed, health, armour, magic resistance, all damage, shorter cooldown). Keep new relics universal and simple.
- Hero reference art: read `production/hero-art/README.md`. The owner generates four views per hero (main, left, back, right) plus accessories with GPT Image through Codex, stored under `art-source/asset-studio/<hero_id>/inputs/references/four_view_r001/`; run `tools/vnext/sync_hero_art.py` afterwards. Never self-approve an image set.
- The creature IDs no longer exist in the catalogue or live source. The ported heroes borrow the retired creature portraits and preview models as placeholders; do not produce further creature art.
- `roster_recipe` (`WonderVNextRosterCatalog()`) adds mana for cast abilities and the executed traits (seven stat traits at first: Human, Orc, Beastkin, Dragonkin, Tank, Fighter, Mage; all 20 since the adoption programme). It is the game's default rule set since 2026-10-03; `-WCControlCatalog` selects the timer-driven, trait-free control used by the native comparison tests.
- Still open (as of 2026-10-10; see the adoption progress line below): every hero and trait is executable, but balance is untuned, the deviations listed in `docs/vnext/adoption/README.md` stand, and there is no owner approval of the new hero art and models. Creature-specific statements in older sections and in `docs/vnext/` are historical where they conflict with this section. Meshy spending still needs explicit owner scope.
- Monsters (owner direction 2026-10-04/05): monster rounds are 1, 2, 3 and every fifth round to 60, with a boss every tenth round; relic drafts follow the boss rounds (the engine still holds three relic slots, so only the first three bosses add a relic until the owner's later relic discussion). Sixteen monsters exist in `neutrals`, eleven with skills borrowed from hero abilities; numbers are untested starting targets. Design: `production/monsters/MONSTER_ROUNDS_DESIGN.md`. A wave may hold at most six monsters (engine rule).
- Monster models use the owner's scripted-Blender route, not Meshy: `tools/blender/monster_kit.py` and `tools/blender/build_monsters.py` build model, baked colour, skeleton and six clips into `art-source/asset-studio/wc_vn_n_<id>/blender_k001/`; they import to `/Game/WonderChess/VNext/Monsters/<Name>_k001` and are listed in `hero_presentation.json` like heroes. This is an owner exception to the no-Blender creature route below. Candidate art, not approved.
- Public catalogue site: `web/catalogue/`, data generated by `tools/vnext/build_catalogue_site.py`, deployed at https://wonder-chess.vercel.app (Vercel project `wonder-chess`). Hero art is written by `tools/vnext/sync_hero_art.py` to `web/catalogue/art/<hero_id>/{main,left,right,back}.webp` (transparent cut-out, 768 px) plus `-thumb.webp` (384 px) for the grid; site-only plain wording lives in `tools/vnext/catalogue_site_copy.json`.

## Owner adoption programme — 2026-10-10

- The owner accepted the design in `docs/vnext/adoption/` (start with its `README.md`). It completes the 13 unimplemented traits, the true abilities of the eleven stand-ins and the 15 planned heroes in nine slices (S1–S9), each adding one engine capability and shipping the traits and heroes it unlocks.
- Progress (keep current): Phase 0 done 2026-10-10 (`docs/vnext/adoption/PHASE_0_REPORT.md`). Slices S1 (effect chains of up to four effects; Stitcher) and S2 (timed modifiers on any stat, damage against negative armour, Undead trait; Bone Archer and Curse Weaver playable, Jinxer and Ironwall given their planned effects) implemented 2026-10-10 with native tests; S3 (fight-start and per-second trait effects, traits with several parts; Dwarf, Priest, Engineer, Sniper, Elemental and Goblin traits) followed the same day. S4 (a seeded per-fight chance stream; evasion, crit, lifesteal, bonus true damage, reflect; Elf, Warlock and Demon traits and the crit half of Assassin; Scaleguard playable) followed the same day. S5 (repeating effects: damage over time, ground zones and auras that follow the caster; Hellblade and Pit Lord playable, Dartblower's poison and Foxclaw's bleed) followed the same day. S6 (target shapes: farthest enemy, densest area, random areas, all allies, line, cone, chain; Rifleman, Siege Master, Forge King, Longbow Scout and Bonechanter given their planned abilities; Dawn Oracle, Flame Regent and Storm Spirit playable) and S7 (statuses hex, silence and taunt, whose magnitude is their duration per star, and mana drain; Shaman trait; Owl Seer and Void Wyrm playable; Ironwall's taunt) followed the same day. S8 (blink as a chained effect with a cell selector, guaranteed critical hits, the Assassin leap at fight start; Moonblade and Foxclaw given their planned abilities, Shadow Fiend playable) and S9 (summoned units from the catalogue's `summons` list, a limit of 4 alive per side, lifetimes, inert walls, fallen-unit cells; Summoner trait; Treant Caller's treant, Wolf Caller, Necromancer and Stone Shaper playable) followed the same day. All 40 heroes and all 20 traits are executable (balance version `wonder_vnext_silkmother_0.11.0` at that point); numbers are untuned and nothing has been viewed in the running game. Summoned units are not survivors and have no models. Phase 4 followed on 2026-10-11 at balance version `wonder_vnext_silkmother_0.12.0`: streak income (3 / 5 / 7 player fights in a row pay 1 / 2 / 3 gold; a seat's streak is saved, save format 4), preparation 25 / 30 / 35 s by stage after a 60 s first round, and auto-deploy of benched units when preparation ends. Loss damage was measured (table in `docs/vnext/adoption/README.md`) and the owner chose 3 / 6 / 9 / 12 on 2026-10-11, applied as balance version `wonder_vnext_silkmother_0.13.0`: 39 median rounds and a 41-minute player-time ceiling in bot tournaments, 87% of matches inside the 35–45 minute target; few seats now reach the round-40 boss and almost none the round-50 boss. `run_vnext_tournament.ps1 -LossDamage '4,7,10,14'` measures another value without editing the catalogue. Phase 5 followed the same day: the fight chart reads the viewer's side, splits damage by type, has a Blocked view and a Mine/Theirs switch and keeps the last fight in the Recap (`Public/Simulation/WonderDamageChart.h`); cues for statuses, damage over time, zones, lines, cones, chains, blinks and summons; and the guided first match (catalogue `tutorial` key, six lessons in English and Indonesian, `Match::BeginGuidedStage`, offered once to a new player and always in the Menu; `-WCGuideAutoPlay` plays it by script with a screenshot per step). The chart, the zone outline and the whole guide were looked at in the running game through scripted screenshots; whether people understand the guide is untested. Next: the cost-curve and ability-weight tuning pass, bot follow-ups, and whatever the owner raises after playing.
- Owner decisions of 2026-10-10: single-target abilities deal about a third of a same-cost health bar; cost-5 shop odds at level 10 are 12% and free experience stays 2; preparation is 25/30/35 s by stage and late loss damage rises so matches end near round 38; shops stay independent; summoned units do not count as survivors; crit and evasion use a seeded per-fight stream; Undead −10/−20 enemy armour, Sniper +10%/+20% basic damage with its range, Engineer +3/+6 mana per second, Shaman hex 3 s; benched units are auto-placed at fight start; new interface text is English and Indonesian.
- Balance measurement: run `tests/runtime/run_vnext_tournament.ps1 -RuleSet roster -Tournaments 200 -OutputDirectory support/runs/balance/<name>` with `.venv/Scripts` on PATH, then `tools/vnext/balance_report.py --run <dir> --output reports/vnext/balance/<name> --baseline <earlier report.json>`. Bot roles come from `roster_recipe.class_roles`; `roster_recipe.bot_spends_surplus` makes bots spend gold above the interest cap. Known limit: bots still field mostly cost-1 boards and hoard gold late, so late bosses and expensive heroes are under-tested.
- Auto Chess reference data lives under `support/reference/autochess/` (git-ignored). Numbers and rules may inform tuning; never copy its names, text, art or code into the repository.
- Approved and applied 2026-10-10 (balance 0.2.1): Ironwall health 850, Forge King 1050, Tide Caller 1070; cost-5 shop odds 3% / 6% / 12% at levels 8 / 9 / 10. Approved and applied 2026-10-11 (balance 0.13.0): loss damage 3 / 6 / 9 / 12 by stage. Not approved: any relic cap change.
- To look at the running game: `UnrealEditor.exe game/WonderChess.uproject -game -WCSolo -WCStorybook -WCProfileName=wonder_vnext -windowed -WCSoloAutoPlay -WCAutoRounds=<n> -WCEvidenceDir=<dir> -WCSavePath=<scratch save>` plays seat 0 by script and writes `auto-rNN-*.png` screenshots with the interface; it logs `WC_AUTO_COMPLETE` when done and must then be closed. The editor module cannot be rebuilt while it runs.
