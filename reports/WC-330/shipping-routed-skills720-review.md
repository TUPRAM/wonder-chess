# Routed Shipping — 720p active-recovery still review

All 21 available active-recovery screenshots from the current routed Shipping run were opened and visually inspected: eleven host captures and ten client captures. Their companion `skill-shots.jsonl` records were read and matched to the images. The archived originals and exact metadata are in `shipping-routed-skills720/`; `shipping-routed-skills720-review-evidence.json` records image hashes, state, positive HP, round, observed seat and release/request ticks.

This is actual packaged 1280×720 gameplay at five-times simulation speed. The observed final sessions report network modes 2/3 and `complete=true` at round23. Those are read session fields; the integration lane owns the actual 2H6B match/network acceptance and its fuller checks. This report certifies only the visual review stated below.

These match fields come from the match-1-qualified session files. The generic client `session.json` later changed to network mode0 after leaving the completed match. That later observation is also preserved in the evidence but is not substituted for the match-qualified record.

The screenshot request triggers on the first observed active-skill recovery for each definition. All21 corresponding records contain state6 and positive HP. Requests occur zero to five simulation ticks after `releaseTick`; the image comes from the next rendered frame. Thus these are real recovery-state observations, not a frame-exact release movie. The existence of an `active-def` screenshot is not sufficient evidence that every release effect or every part of an animation is visible in it.

## Actual findings

Hero scale, equipment attachment, family proportions and costume grouping remain coherent during real combat. The header/shop camera framing stays clear of the ordinary board. No gross mesh deformation, shrink, rotated basis, missing model or detached weapon is apparent. The revised low Defeat posture is also visible on fallen characters in several client images, while living characters continue their actions nearby.

Gold shield rings and their labels are clearly present, including host definitions0/1 and client definition0. Stun indicators appear in host definition6 and several client frames. Thin blue travel cues appear in host definitions3/5/7/8/11 and client definitions3/5/7. Faint violet area edges and small effect motes appear in client definitions1/2/4/6/8/9. These effects are visible without a large bloom washout or a large opaque screen cover. Thick shield rings partially cover torso detail while keeping the head and team base visible.

The small gold status text and pale violet edges have modest contrast against limestone at720p, and fine equipment contact remains difficult to resolve. This is a minor readability/polish limitation rather than evidence of a missing skill. In some recovery captures, especially those of caster actions, the target skill's distinct release burst is no longer clearly separable from other ongoing effects. No all-skill visual timing pass is inferred. The upcoming normal-speed1080p capture must establish stronger timing evidence before that gate can close.

Some early images show the actual `That action is unavailable during battle` rejection notice caused by the scripted input exercise. It occupies the status strip and does not obscure the board. The message is retained in the original screenshots; it is not described as a rendering failure.
