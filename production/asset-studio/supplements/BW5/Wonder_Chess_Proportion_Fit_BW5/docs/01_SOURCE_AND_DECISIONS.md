## What the supplied report establishes

The fixed glove is a derivative of the licensed open glove, not a frozen BW2/BW3 failed animation. The retained hand cage has 1,185 vertices and 1,191 faces. The worst handle intrusion is at the middle proximal/metacarpal base, not at a distal pad. The final evaluated observations are 141 self-crossing pairs, 527 handle pairs and 30 guard pairs; the worst sampled handle penetration is 11.814 mm. Pair counts are not counts of independent defects.

The canonical 27-bone rig was inspected and contains no digit bones. The seven old actions retain hand/equipment relationships across 865 source samples. The NEW hand's new orientation and new armor have not been tested in those clips. ## What this handoff adds as a proposal

1. Armor is the first execution lane; it no longer waits for a grip gate.
2. Fitting has three independent constraints: the reference silhouette, the actual padded-body envelope, and supported motion. None can substitute for another.
3. Front proportions are measured approximately from THIS uploaded image. Unknown three-dimensional dimensions stay unknown. The numbers are starting review guides, not approved template dimensions.
4. The front and back use different hem shapes. Collar and waist underlayers are part of the torso composition, not decorative work deferred indefinitely.
5. The hand repair changes the failing root/web construction around an explicit hilt keep-out volume. It does not repeat the same interpolation with new coefficients.

## Reference priority and ambiguity

Use the new four-view sheet for costume proportions and layer design. Keep the primary portrait for face identity. Refer to older written decisions where the generated sheet is inconsistent. Do not silently read a side-view label as proof of anatomical handedness; identify actual subject orientation.

Painted highlights do not always represent physical seams. Side arms, hair, straps and cap overlaps conceal portions of the armor. Do not infer a hidden rear-shell curve, skin width or torso circumference from those pixels. Record a deliberate design completion where the reference is incomplete.
