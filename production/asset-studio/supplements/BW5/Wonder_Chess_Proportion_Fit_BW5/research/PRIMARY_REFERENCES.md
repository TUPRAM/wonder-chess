# Primary technical references consulted for BW5

These explain available operations. They do not establish that those operations produced a successful Ada. BW5's source report records Blender 5.1.1; pin the actual installed editor and verify current RNA/UI rather than upgrading to the version shown by a web page.

| ID | Primary source | Supported point | Limits |
|---|---|---|---|
| B1 | [Blender camera documentation](https://docs.blender.org/manual/en/4.1/render/cameras.html) and [camera projection discussion](https://docs.blender.org/manual/id/4.3/render/cameras.html) | Orthographic projection, scale, and Fit versus Stretch image behavior | Does not make an illustrated reference truly orthographic |
| B2 | [Blender Lattice modifier](https://docs.blender.org/manual/en/dev/modeling/modifiers/deform/lattice.html) | Editable broad deformation and optional vertex-group influence | Does not enforce collision, shape or body fit |
| B3 | [Blender Shrinkwrap](https://docs.blender.org/manual/en/2.80/modeling/modifiers/deform/shrinkwrap.html) | Target, offset, groups, projection axis/limit and inside/outside caveat | Older reference for concepts; verify installed names and behavior; not a reliable substitute for a collision solver |
| B4 | [Blender Solidify](https://docs.blender.org/manual/id/5.2/modeling/modifiers/generate/solidify.html) | Thickness in local coordinates, scale effects, offset/rim behavior | Do not upgrade from the working editor or change protected garment settings automatically |
| B5 | [Blender Bridge Edge Loops](https://docs.blender.org/manual/th/5.1/modeling/meshes/editing/edge/bridge_edge_loops.html) | Loop pairing, correspondence/twist and interpolation choices | Does not ensure bridges remain outside a hilt |
| B6 | [Epic: Skeletal Mesh Sockets](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/en-us/unreal-engine/skeletal-mesh-sockets-in-unreal-engine) | Bone-relative attachment and mesh-specific socket options | A socket does not solve anatomy, armor motion or bind-space mismatch |

Source status: official pages were read/search-reviewed during preparation. Some English 5.1 manual endpoints failed to fetch; accessible official edition/localization pages above supplied the narrowly cited concepts. No video tutorial, asset purchase, download, or live tool execution is claimed.

All construction choices, ratios, source interpretations and proposed acceptance rules not directly supported by the supplied reports are labeled as BW5 proposals or manual image observations. No new historical armor claim is used to override the user's design.
