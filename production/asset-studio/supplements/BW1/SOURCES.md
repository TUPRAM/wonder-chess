# Sources and evidence boundary

Online pages checked 9 September 2026. These support the cited project/tool facts; the proposed Ada construction steps, research phases, budgets and acceptance rules are our recommendations. Downloaded artifact licenses and actual tool compatibility still require local checking.

## mpfb_license
MakeHuman / MPFB split license

https://static.makehumancommunity.org/about/license.html

Retain the already installed core foundation. External asset packs are separate provenance entries.

## mpfb_generator
MakeHuman: building a character generator from system assets

https://static.makehumancommunity.org/mpfb/faq/build_other_chargen.html

The project explicitly discusses reuse of system assets in another character generator. Review any code reuse separately.

## mpfb_assets
MPFB asset creation tools

https://static.makehumancommunity.org/mpfb/docs/assets/asset_creation_intro.html

Integrated tools for targets, garments, materials, rigs and poses. Verify the installed-version interface.

## mpfb_clothes
MPFB Creating clothes

https://static.makehumancommunity.org/mpfb/docs/assets/creating_clothes.html

Use a helper-derived garment template. The documented one-group rule concerns fitting correspondence, not final skeletal influence count.

## mpfb_export
MPFB Export copy

https://static.makehumancommunity.org/mpfb/docs/exporting/export_copy.html

Create a separate export object hierarchy; choose masks, helpers and subdivision treatment deliberately.

## mpfb_hands
Hands01

https://static.makehumancommunity.org/assets/assetpacks/hands01.html

Targets include mindfront_hand_fingers_correction, mindfront_hand_thenar_eminence and mindfront_hand_hypothenar. These are not separate hand meshes.

## mpfb_gloves
Gloves01

https://static.makehumancommunity.org/assets/assetpacks/gloves01.html

Inspect toigo_gloves_short by MargaretToigo and culturalibre_hero-heroine_gloves_1 first; do not import the entire pack by default.

## mpfb_shoes
Shoes01

https://static.makehumancommunity.org/assets/assetpacks/shoes01.html

Inspect culturalibre_hero_boots_1 and a toigo_ankle_boots variant. Select a flat practical silhouette after actual inspection.

## blender_bases
Blender Human Base Meshes v1.4.1

https://www.blender.org/download/demo-files/

Official listing specifies Blender 4.2 LTS or newer. Other demo assets on the same page have other licenses. Search-result listing accessible; page fetch failed in this session.

## blender_bases_download
Blender Human Base Meshes official archive index

https://download.blender.org/demo/asset-bundles/human-base-meshes/

Official archive index corroborates the v1.4.1 ZIP. No archive downloaded or inspected here.

## quaternius_bases
Quaternius Universal Base Characters

https://quaternius.com/packs/universalbasecharacters.html

The page distinguishes 60–70% free in OBJ/FBX/glTF from full Source-tier contents and .blend files. Do not promise every catalog item or native source for free.

## quaternius_fantasy
Quaternius Modular Character Outfits — Fantasy

https://quaternius.com/packs/modularcharacteroutfitsfantasy.html

The complete catalog lists 12 outfits / 62 parts. Exact free contents must be inspected. Not automatically compatible with MPFB proportions or skeleton.

## knight_cc0
Knight medieval no textures — DmytroKovkun

https://blendswap.com/blend/13794

Blender 2.7x; creator says no rig or textures. Login required for download. Isolated study source, not an approved runtime asset.

## gambeson
Gambeson — Leon Steiner

https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/gambeson-0fc44d2876ed449ca9473e78c83a8d39

Attribution-bearing optional construction reference. Author reports mixed-tool workflow; a final mesh does not expose its complete operation history. Not included in the initial training set.

## polyhaven
Poly Haven license

https://polyhaven.com/license

Optional surface source. Asset rights do not imply that every website service or bulk-access route is unrestricted.

## ambientcg
ambientCG license

https://docs.ambientcg.com/license/

Optional cloth/leather/metal material source, not anatomical geometry.

## solidify
Blender Solidify modifier

https://docs.blender.org/manual/en/latest/modeling/modifiers/generate/solidify.html

Thickness/offset and scale must be inspected; the modifier is not a universal collision or exact-thickness guarantee.

## data_transfer
Blender Data Transfer modifier

https://docs.blender.org/manual/en/4.3/modeling/modifiers/modify/data_transfer.html

Vertex-group transfer is an initialization method; inspect mapping, masks and rest-pose alignment.

## ue_modular
Epic: Working with Modular Characters

https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/en-us/unreal-engine/working-with-modular-characters-in-unreal-engine

Modular authoring does not require many separately rendered runtime components. Evaluate Leader Pose / Copy Pose / merge tradeoffs in the installed engine.

## codex_skills
OpenAI: Codex skills

https://developers.openai.com/codex/skills/

Skills package instructions, resources and optional scripts. They are not automatically neural-weight training.

## openai_training_status
OpenAI fine-tuning platform status notice

https://openai.com/index/introducing-improvements-to-the-fine-tuning-api-and-expanding-our-custom-models-program/

The May 8, 2026 update says the platform is winding down and closed to new users. Do not make future availability an architectural dependency.

## triposr
TripoSR — original VAST-AI-Research repository

https://github.com/VAST-AI-Research/TripoSR

Default single-image inference is documented at about 6 GB VRAM. No inference, installation, benchmarking or output licensing audit performed here.

## trellis2
TRELLIS.2 — Microsoft

https://github.com/microsoft/TRELLIS.2

Official tested setup: Linux, NVIDIA GPU with at least 24 GB memory. Optional future part-candidate generator, not an approved rigged-character pipeline.

## objaverse
Objaverse 1.0 documentation

https://objaverse.allenai.org/docs/objaverse-1.0/

A dataset index does not give one blanket license to all source objects. Filter provenance and rights before any training ingestion.
