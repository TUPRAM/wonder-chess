# First pilot: Ada likeness, not full-roster regeneration

## Current evidence and boundary

The user supplied a polished multi-view Ada illustration and a smoother but visually rejected 3D render. Those images are included in `examples/ada/reference_inputs/`. They are not rendered outputs of this kit. Native crops simply expose existing details; no new reference images were generated here.

The illustration's front is angled, equipment occludes anatomy, and some views vary in garment/hair treatment. The recent render is cropped above the feet. Neither is a perfect measurement blueprint. Do not derive a rigorous whole-body silhouette match from those incompatible inputs.

## Identity to preserve

Ada is an adult human guardian with warm brown skin, calm alert expression, defined brows, swept dark hair leading into a braid, protective shoulder silhouette, ivory underlayer, navy garment panels, steel armor and a gold sun shield. Preserve shield on anatomical left and sword on right; resolve screen-side mirroring explicitly. Existing gameplay ID and self-shield ability remain unchanged.

## Decisions to resolve

Choose the exact hairstyle, braid length, armor segmentation, tabard hem and trim level from the selected visual. The original simplified text and richer illustration may conflict. Pram approves a small reconciliation, not another entire world bible. Keep the sun motif consistent between shield, accessories and UI; do not invent extra ornament to fill blank surfaces.

## First task packet

Use the full illustration as identity context; selected head closeup for facial structure/expression; top-row body panels as non-orthographic construction candidates; isolated sword/shield for equipment design. The crop manifest records source bounds. The rejected render is only a discrepancy baseline.

Required missing references: unarmed neutral front and profile, left/right hand construction and grip, armor/collar section and side/back garment decisions. Use anchored image generation if available and approved; otherwise author a simple measured 3D blockout and render construction views. Label invented hidden surfaces as design choices.

## Method proof

Do not run the bulk generator as the final model-authoring method merely because it can complete all files. Inspect existing geometry and decide which parts, if any, provide a useful base. A suitable approved stylized base head can save reconstruction work; an image-to-3D candidate is a separate tested option, not accepted automatically.

Produce only head, neck, collar, chest and one representative shoulder in clay. Fix eye-lid relationship, brow/cheek/jaw construction, closed-mouth expression, swept hair masses, armor planes and layering. The body/shoulder form must not remain a smooth inflated cap. Show front, true profile and three-quarter with a face crop at native resolution.

## Reject conditions

- A generic face with white dots/patches instead of coherent eyes/lids.
- A small dark mouth opening unrelated to the selected expression.
- A ball-like crest or spherical shoulder used where a constructed plate is intended.
- Color/lighting polish hiding wrong anatomy or missing layers.
- A report claiming approval without actual image inspection.
- One flattering view concealing a broken profile or missing back surfaces.

## After forms approval

Complete rest of body, refined hands, cloth/hair, shield and sword. Test untextured assembly and grip before UVs. Then follow retopology, early deformation, UV/bake/materials, formal rig/skin, clips, LODs and Unreal. Keep the old playable asset while the new one is a candidate; do not break the game to show progress.

Pram has three mandatory art decisions: reference/interpretation, likeness/forms, final in-game candidate. Routine technical iterations remain with Codex/reviewers. After Ada passes, validate a contrasting slender/cloth-led hero and a stocky hero before attempting the rest of the roster.
