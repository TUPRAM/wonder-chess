**Decision: reject promotion.** Root75 improves one metric but fails the adopted below-2% screen and produces substantially more capped tournaments. This does not support simply reducing healing again or adding wait time. Fixed-army and mirrored Cragstoat fixtures distinguish approach opportunity from raw damage; normal acquisition cost and explicit failed-charge reasons still need stronger evidence. Reserved confirmation seeds 900001–900250 remain unused.

The separate final solo catalogue, which retains control healing and introduces the owned-team relic-offer policy, completed 1,000 native tournaments with 186,984 encounters, 26,219 timeouts (**14.022%**), 187 capped tournaments and a 31.361-minute median accelerated simulated duration. Its below-2% timeout screen also **fails**. Exact native/engine agreement certifies implementation consistency for this catalogue; it does not turn this balance result into acceptance or supply human duration evidence.

The next balance work should classify cap-heavy survivors, effective heal versus overheal, charge opportunity/commitment/denial and early counter access; choose a bounded targeting/movement/interaction experiment before another damage adjustment. Keep the prior tuning control while independently testing UI improvements. Raw comparison and protocol: `reports/vnext/milestones/b1-balance/`.
