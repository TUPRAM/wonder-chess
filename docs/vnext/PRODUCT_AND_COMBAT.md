# Product, combat and content contract

## Player experience

The central pleasure is seeing a prepared formation produce a surprising but understandable battle. Recruitment, deployment, orientation, relic assignment and scouting occur during preparation. The battle resolves automatically through authoritative C++; the player learns from it and changes the next formation. Telegraphs communicate commitments rather than ask for manual dodging.

Successful teams combine at least eight strategic families: protected carries, momentum rush, positional sustain, artillery, backline pressure, constructions, displacement and isolated duels. A hero earns a roster place by adding a decision, counter, acquisition bridge, body family or meaningful alternative. Membership is not a race/class cross-product. Freeze accepted membership for each release candidate, then continue development in a new content version.

## Tournament and economy

Eight seats recruit on an 8×8 encounter board using four local preparation rows per side. Side B rotates both row and column. Each recruited unit occupies one cell, including large creatures and flyers. Visual flight never confers untargetability or ignores occupancy. Deployment capacity follows levels 3–10; the bench holds ten units; shops contain five independent offers across five costs. Three equal copies merge into the next star through three stars, retaining deterministic identity and location. The source owns all numeric tuning.

Shop tier probabilities are integer basis points totaling 10,000 per level, then a uniform draw among enabled heroes at the chosen cost. Draws are independent with replacement. Opponent purchases never deplete supply. The six-hero laboratory covers all five cost tiers to prevent impossible draws, but its small supply creates acquisition patterns that cannot represent a finished ecosystem.

Retain atomic ownership-, sequence-, phase- and revision-checked commands for buy, sell, move, swap, XP, reroll, lock and ready. Add local facing and relic transactions through that authority. Public scouting exposes deployed formation, traits, relics, standings and the next opponent; benches, shop offers and draft choices remain private. Rejected commands cannot alter resources, facing or equipment.

Initial neutral rounds are 1–3, then every fifth round. Their final mechanics teach screening, spreading and protecting support, followed by milestone bosses. Current basic-combat layouts are executable lab fixtures; boss skills and learning acceptance are separate work. Per-round gold, XP, health loss and clocks are provisional versioned values. Record actual battle duration separately from preparation and settlement; do not obtain the duration target by unmeasured waiting.

Solo interruption support pauses the authoritative world, saves a versioned snapshot at preparation boundaries, and resumes with the same source identity and deterministic streams. Incompatible content or corrupt snapshots must fail clearly and preserve the existing save. Profile-specific saves may not be mistaken for legacy saves.

Use integer centipoints, basis points and quantized milliseconds as in the existing deterministic core. Released packets survive source defeat; dead recipients are ineligible for healing. A committed unreleased cast can be interrupted. Event order and random streams are shared by observed and off-screen combat. Source tuning and measured tests decide exact ranges, durations, magnitudes and delays; no dossier or widget maintains a second balance table.

Persistent areas, constructions, summons, reactions and transformations are introduced only with focused scenario tests and visible ownership. Cap summoned entities at four per side when summons are implemented. They never contribute to recruited-type synergy thresholds. Transformations retain one unit identity and economy value. No unrestricted spell copying, recursive summons or general-purpose interaction explosion is part of this release plan.

## Relics and progression

Survivors draft one relic from three offers after rounds 3, 15 and 25, regardless of the neutral result. A team equips at most three, one per hero. Reassignment occurs only during preparation. Selling returns the relic to its owner's inventory. A merge preserves the deterministic survivor's relic and returns others. Draft ownership, offers and selected results are deterministic, private and included in compatible saves.

The initial executable relic catalogue uses bounded ability transformations: magnitude, range, radius, cast time, cooldown and duration. Each design has at least two compatible implemented mechanics and an actual drawback. Transform the original canonical ability exactly once, then clamp and quantize through shared evaluation. Do not apply transforms cumulatively during repeated inspection or equip/unequip. Future split rescues and construction exchanges need dedicated runtime behavior; they are not simulated through misleading generic stat changes.

Progression outside a match records bestiary discoveries, mastery, achievements, match history, saved formation sketches and offline challenge seeds. Rewards unlock expression and learning, never permanent competitive combat power. These systems remain backlog items until implemented and verified; no runtime LLM or paid service is needed.

## Content acceptance

Every dossier answers decision, positioning, allies, counters and recognition. It includes a distinct silhouette/body family and production status. Every hero must have a useful formation, an unfavorable one, practical counters, meaningful star comparisons and a normal shop acquisition path. Every substantial trait changes a behavior or formation incentive. Supporting numeric modifiers may accompany that behavior; inactive behavioral designs must never display as completed synergy bonuses.

Generated coverage lists which thresholds lack enough authored members. Adding members must preserve asymmetry and prevent redundant choices. The current fourteen candidates and six executable heroes do not satisfy full roster or eight-archetype release acceptance.
