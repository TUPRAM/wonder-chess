# Aurelune — Wonder Chess World Brief

Recruit heroes from a hopeful fantasy world, form alliances through race and class, and outplay seven rival captains on a magical board.

## Why captains compete

The Dawn Compact runs enchanted field trials to prepare mixed teams for the encroaching Hollow Court. Arena manifestations dissolve safely when defeated; competitive matches are training contests, not the entire war. Duplicate recruits and star upgrades are explicit game abstractions rather than a cloning economy.

## The threat

The Hollow Court is a supernatural host crossing damaged wards beyond Cindercrest. Its leaders seek control of the old beacon network. Playable peoples are not inherently aligned with it; the threat is an organization and its choices, not a race.

## Brighthaven Vale

Location: central southwest. Neighbors: thistlewood, stoneveil, willowrun.

Settlement: Sunbridge. Landmark: The Seven-Lantern Courtyard.

Everyday life: The Copper Kettle tavern.

Visual identity: Warm limestone, blue roofs, hanging gardens, oak beams and sunlit market cloth.

Culture: Patrol captains, farmers, students and traveling craftspeople negotiate common road duties.

What is at stake: Keep bridges and market routes open so the alliance can supply distant settlements.

Associated heroes: Ada Brightshield, Mira Dawnwell, Rowan Emberwick, Cass Vale.

## Thistlewood Reach

Location: northwest. Neighbors: brighthaven, stoneveil.

Settlement: Moonbough. Landmark: The Listening Observatory.

Everyday life: A canopy tea garden serving visitors from several peoples.

Visual identity: Ancient broadleaf trunks, curved wooden galleries, turquoise ponds and restrained luminous flowers.

Culture: Forest stewardship and public songs carry local histories; visitors trade and learn rather than being automatically excluded.

What is at stake: Preserve the forest road and understand the changes in the night sky.

Associated heroes: Liora Leafstep, Elin Moonsong, Sylas Duskrun, Neris Starbloom.

## Stoneveil Deeps

Location: north central under the mountains. Neighbors: thistlewood, brighthaven, stormstep.

Settlement: Hearthhall. Landmark: The Bell Bridge.

Everyday life: Orla’s shared hall and open workshops.

Visual identity: Large stone vaults, amber furnace windows, bronze railings and cultivated subterranean gardens.

Culture: Craft guilds judge useful work and reliable public infrastructure; residents include migrants and travelers.

What is at stake: Repair the old routes and send dependable materials to the frontier.

Associated heroes: Borin Stonebell, Tessa Brassbolt, Dagna Anvilheart, Orla Hearthglow.

## Stormstep Highlands

Location: northeast. Neighbors: stoneveil, cindercrest, willowrun.

Settlement: Highpass. Landmark: The Wind Stair.

Everyday life: Caravan courtyards with woven awnings.

Visual identity: Sun-ochre stone, grassy terraces, broad geometric textiles and clear mountain skies.

Culture: Caravan communities cooperate across long distances and treat navigation and mutual aid as serious skills.

What is at stake: Prevent isolation when dangerous weather and hostile incursions close the passes.

Associated heroes: Rok Sunward, Zura Stormcall, Kesh Quickwind, Tala Ironroot.

## Willowrun Rivers

Location: south central. Neighbors: brighthaven, stormstep, cindercrest.

Settlement: Orchard Bend. Landmark: The Floating Festival Walk.

Everyday life: Riverside bakeries, gardens and boat markets.

Visual identity: Warm plaster, green orchards, colorful cloth, waterwheels and low welcoming bridges.

Culture: Traveling festivals, agriculture and trade connect the wider world rather than making this region merely comic relief.

What is at stake: Maintain food and communications without losing the everyday pleasures the coalition protects.

Associated heroes: Pippa Oakstride, Finn Thistlearrow, Nella Quickpocket, Milo Mistwhistle.

## Cindercrest Heights

Location: east. Neighbors: stormstep, willowrun.

Settlement: Beacon Rest. Landmark: The First Lantern Spire.

Everyday life: Terraced gardens and map rooms beside the beacons.

Visual identity: Pale mountain rock, teal banners, golden beacon housings and prismatic old ruins.

Culture: Beacon keepers and travelers exchange maps, astronomy and maintenance duties.

What is at stake: Seal damaged wards without surrendering the frontier or treating outsiders as enemies.

Associated heroes: Sora Dawnscale, Varek Prismshot, Iri Cinderstep, Oren Skyward.

## Mortal organizations

**Dawn Compact** — Coordinate training, relief and joint defense.

**Greenward Circle** — Maintain forest routes, observatories and shared knowledge.

**Hearthwright Union** — Provide reliable craft, safe halls and infrastructure.

**Highland Watch** — Guide and defend caravans across the passes.

**Wayfarer Fellowship** — Keep trade, food and ordinary community life connected.

**Skyward Conclave** — Care for the beacon network and study the breach.
